Welcome to PenguinCAM

PenguinCAM is a CAM post-processor that generates 3-axis CNC router G-code from DXF files exported from Onshape. Built for FRC Team 6238 Popcorn Penguins, it automates the CAD-to-CNC workflow for flat plates and tubes without requiring CAM software.

Philosophy: FRC doesn't need to teach machining. Most teams do not have traditional machine tools. A (relatively) inexpensive 3-axis CNC router is all an FRC team really needs. But the traditional software to use any sort of CNC machine is fairly daunting. Instead, PenguinCAM has the attitude that students want to get their part out of their CAD screen and into their hands with as little friction as possible, similar to a laser cutter or 3D printer.

To accomplish this simplicity, it takes two strategies:

  • Move the repetitive and opaque tasks into a one-time setup task (create a config file and save it in Onshape classroom) so that team-specific choices are shared by all students on the team, with reasonable defaults so that most teams won't even need to do it.
  • Make the per-part inputs as minimal as possible. It does this by making some FRC-realistic assumptions:
    • The machine is a 3-axis CNC router that speaks standard G-code (e.g. Omio, ShopBot, ShopSabre, Avid, XCarve, and similar)
    • The parts will be cut from a small library of common FRC materials
    • The parts are flat in nature (2D/2.5D plates and faces of tubes)
    • A single tool (often a 4mm end mill) is sufficient to cut all features

This design with these assumptions make it such that the student making parts only has to do minimal manual steps:

  • Pick the part type
  • Identify the part(s) to be cut in CAD
  • Specify per-job variables (material, tool size)
  • Affix the stock to the CNC router and set the X/Y zero point on the stock
  • Load the G-code into the router
  • Press Go!

There might be certain machines for which there are extra steps. Some might require manual moving of the G-code to the router. Some might require re-zeroing the Z axis. Some might require manually starting the spindle or the cooling system. But where available, PenguinCAM will generate gcode to do all of that. PenguinCAM will even move the spindle out of the way at the end of the job for you! If it's a repetitive manual step, PenguinCAM will attempt to automate it for you.

Because there are so few inputs and decisions made by the student, there is very little risk that anything will go wrong. If an end mill breaks, it's almost certainly PenguinCAM's fault, not the student's.

🔗

Runs Inside Onshape

The entire workflow lives in a panel docked to your Onshape window. Click a face in your model and it imports instantly — no exporting, uploading, or switching tabs.

🧩

Multi-Part Jobs

Nest several parts on one sheet of stock and cut them in a single program. Drag to position, rotate, and flip each part on a visual layout canvas.

🎯

Smart Detection

Automatically detects holes, pockets, and perimeters. Configurable tab strategies: auto-place and remove, or pause for fixturing through holes.

📐

Interactive Preview

Visualize your toolpaths in 3D before cutting. Orbit the view, scrub through moves, and animate the tool at adjustable speed.

🔧

Multi-Layer (2.5D)

Cut parts with grooves, pockets, countersinks, and raised bosses at multiple depths. Thickness is read straight from your CAD model.

📦

Tube Machining

Cut patterns on aluminum tubing, with automatic end squaring, cut-to-length, and mirrored or distinct patterns per face.

⚙️

Team Customization

Configure machine dimensions, materials, and cutting parameters for your specific CNC setup with a simple config file.

Quick Start

1

Install the Onshape App

Add PenguinCAM to your Onshape account from the Onshape App Store. This docks a PenguinCAM panel to the side of your Onshape window.

2

Set Up the Job

Open the panel and choose your machine, cutting mode (2D, 2.5D, or tubing), tool, and material.

3

Add Parts

Click a face in your Onshape model — it imports automatically. Add more faces to build a multi-part job, then arrange them on the stock.

4

Preview & Download

Review the 3D toolpath preview, then download your G-code program or save it straight to Google Drive.

Hosted Service

PenguinCAM is hosted at penguincam.popcornpenguins.com and is free to use for any FRC team. We handle the infrastructure, authentication, and updates.

ℹ️
Multi-Team Support: Other teams can use the hosted service by uploading a PenguinCAM-config.yaml file to customize settings for their CNC machine. See Configuration for details.
💡
No Onshape? No problem. If you don't use Onshape, you can still run the full workflow by uploading DXF files directly at penguincam.popcornpenguins.com/app. See How to Use.
