Welcome to PenguinCAM

PenguinCAM is a CAM post-processor that generates CNC G-code from DXF files exported from Onshape. Built for FRC Team 6238 Popcorn Penguins, it automates the CAD-to-CNC workflow for flat plates without requiring CAM software.

🔗

Runs Inside Onshape

The entire workflow lives in a panel docked to your Onshape window. Click a face in your model and it imports instantly — no exporting, uploading, or switching tabs.

🧩

Multi-Part Jobs

Nest several parts on one sheet of stock and cut them in a single program. Drag to position, rotate, and flip each part on a visual layout canvas.

🎯

Smart Detection

Automatically detects holes, pockets, and perimeters. Configurable tab strategies: auto-place and remove, or pause for fixturing through holes.

📐

Interactive Preview

Visualize your toolpaths in 3D before cutting. Orbit the view, scrub through moves, and animate the tool at adjustable speed.

🔧

Multi-Layer (2.5D)

Cut parts with grooves, pockets, countersinks, and raised bosses at multiple depths. Thickness is read straight from your CAD model.

📦

Tube Machining

Cut patterns on aluminum tubing, with automatic end squaring, cut-to-length, and mirrored or distinct patterns per face.

⚙️

Team Customization

Configure machine dimensions, materials, and cutting parameters for your specific CNC setup with a simple config file.

Quick Start

1

Install the Onshape App

Add PenguinCAM to your Onshape account from the Onshape App Store. This docks a PenguinCAM panel to the side of your Onshape window.

2

Set Up the Job

Open the panel and choose your machine, cutting mode (2D, 2.5D, or tubing), tool, and material.

3

Add Parts

Click a face in your Onshape model — it imports automatically. Add more faces to build a multi-part job, then arrange them on the stock.

4

Preview & Download

Review the 3D toolpath preview, then download your G-code program or save it straight to Google Drive.

Hosted Service

PenguinCAM is hosted at penguincam.popcornpenguins.com and is free to use for any FRC team. We handle the infrastructure, authentication, and updates.

ℹ️
Multi-Team Support: Other teams can use the hosted service by uploading a PenguinCAM-config.yaml file to customize settings for their CNC machine. See Configuration for details.
💡
No Onshape? No problem. If you don't use Onshape, you can still run the full workflow by uploading DXF files directly at penguincam.popcornpenguins.com/app. See How to Use.
